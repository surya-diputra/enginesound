What the app does

SDMV EngineSound plays a synthesized engine sound through your phone or a connected speaker, following your speed (RIDE) or your own throttle (REV). It is entertainment. It is not a safety device and does not replace a vehicle's own warning sounds.

Look after your speakers and your hearing

  • Start low and raise the volume slowly. Engine sound has strong bass that can damage small speakers at high volume.
  • For a motorcycle's own speaker or another small speaker, choose the LOUD speaker preset: it leaves out the deep bass small speakers cannot play.
  • Stop if the sound distorts or rattles. Long listening at high volume can damage your hearing.

Every speaker has its own limits. You decide which speaker the app plays through and how loud, and you are responsible for keeping within what that speaker can take.

Riding safely and legally

  • Set the app up before you ride. Do not handle your phone while riding.
  • Keep the volume at a level where you can still hear traffic, horns and emergency vehicles.
  • Rules on vehicle sound, noise and the use of phones while riding differ from place to place. You are responsible for following the rules where you ride.
  • Speed shown in the app comes from your phone's GPS and motion sensors. It can be wrong or late; never use it in place of your vehicle's speedometer.
