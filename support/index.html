SDMV EngineSound Support

SDMV EngineSound plays a live, synthesized engine sound for your electric motorcycle. It follows your speed from GPS in RIDE mode, or your hand on the on-screen throttle, brake and gears in REV mode.

Before you ride

  • Pair a Bluetooth speaker or use the phone speaker, choose an engine and press Play before you set off.
  • Allow location access so RIDE mode can follow your speed. The sound keeps playing with the screen off.
  • Keep your eyes on the road. Do not use the app's screen while riding, and follow local noise rules.

Common questions

The engine stays at idle while I ride. RIDE mode needs a GPS fix. Wait a few seconds outdoors, and check that location access is allowed for the app.

How do I unlock all engines? Tap an engine marked PRO and choose Unlock Pro. It is a one-time purchase. Use Restore purchase on a new phone with the same store account.

Can I change the engine without unlocking the screen? Yes. Use the previous and next buttons on the lock screen, in Control Center or on your headset or speaker.
